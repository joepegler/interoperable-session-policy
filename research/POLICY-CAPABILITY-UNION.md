# Policy Capability Union

## Status

This is the observed capability union at the pinned source revisions. It is an input to implementer review, not a permanent registry, endorsement of every capability, or allocation of final type identifiers.

Each row has exactly one current classification:

1. **Candidate mandatory baseline**: the narrow requirement appears useful, displayable, and credibly portable enough to test with implementers.
2. **Candidate standard extension**: the semantics are potentially reusable, but support should not be required for baseline conformance.
3. **Implementation-specific**: the mechanism belongs to an account, binding, transport, or application rather than the portable authority vocabulary.
4. **Unresolved**: evidence or implementer agreement is insufficient to place or define the capability safely.

The classifications separate a semantic requirement from its mechanism. For example, committing to the complete granted meaning is a baseline requirement, while the canonical codec, hash, domain, and collection ordering remain unresolved. Likewise, a portable cost quota is a separate question from binding-specific payer or paymaster selection.

### E-identity: account, chain, and actor

- `E-ID-01`: EIP-8130 [`Actor Policies`](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/blob/dbfa6bee8329650969b95080f23f7059c015c2ba/EIPS/eip-8130.md#actor-policies), Smart Sessions v1 [`Session`](https://github.com/erc7579/smartsessions/blob/f5aaf867f7e22f3b9d746ce6f404f3a56833757f/contracts/DataTypes.sol), Kernel [`ValidationManager`](https://github.com/zerodevapp/kernel/blob/f2a84a332ec5a722e7e95a0d64601905c3c87fe9/src/core/ValidationManager.sol), and MetaMask [`Delegation`](https://github.com/MetaMask/delegation-framework/blob/9e7fd80dc5a1f74bcb5efdff6aa91b228fb86411/src/utils/Types.sol) all bind authority to an account-side identity or delegator, although their signed domains and installation models differ.
- `E-ID-02`: EIP-8130 supports multiple authenticator forms in [`Keystore.sol`](https://github.com/base/eip-8130/blob/bc41a9715d0eecc3fdd27a6d9ed422aa4a151f7b/src/Keystore.sol); Smart Sessions accepts a typed validator and initialisation data in [`DataTypes.sol`](https://github.com/erc7579/smartsessions/blob/f5aaf867f7e22f3b9d746ce6f404f3a56833757f/contracts/DataTypes.sol); Kernel exposes a concrete [`ECDSASigner`](https://github.com/zerodevapp/kernel-7579-plugins/blob/332deed6eeef3d6279cde50aa1d51eff53728bd4/src/signers/ECDSASigner.sol).
- `E-ID-03`: EIP-8130 commits its EIP-155 chain ID in the [`Signature Payload`](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/blob/dbfa6bee8329650969b95080f23f7059c015c2ba/EIPS/eip-8130.md#signature-payload); Smart Sessions represents per-chain and multichain authorisation through [`ChainDigest`](https://github.com/erc7579/smartsessions/blob/f5aaf867f7e22f3b9d746ce6f404f3a56833757f/contracts/DataTypes.sol#L14-L27); MetaMask's manager domain records `block.chainid` in [`DelegationManager`](https://github.com/MetaMask/delegation-framework/blob/9e7fd80dc5a1f74bcb5efdff6aa91b228fb86411/src/DelegationManager.sol#L58-L68). These precedents do not solve multichain counters or revocation propagation.

- `E-ACT-04`: Kernel supports single calls, batches, and optional delegatecalls in [`Kernel.sol`](https://github.com/zerodevapp/kernel/blob/f2a84a332ec5a722e7e95a0d64601905c3c87fe9/src/Kernel.sol), with an explicit warning that a fallback delegatecall runs in account storage context. ## Candidate mandatory baseline

These rows are recommendations derived from the evidence, not claims that every inspected implementation already conforms.

| ID | Atomic capability | Evidence | Evidence kind | Required portable meaning | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ENV-01` | One existing account on one EIP-155 chain | `E-ID-01` and `E-ID-03` in [E-identity](#e-identity-account-chain-and-actor) | Inference and recommendation | The granted object contains one exact positive chain ID and one exact nonzero account whose authority is delegated | Required for execution identity and replay separation; multichain is not silently implied |
| `ENV-02` | Address session actor | `E-ID-02` in [E-identity](#e-identity-account-chain-and-actor) | Implementation and recommendation | One exact nonzero address identifies the actor; a binding proves how its validator or authenticator maps to that address | Narrowest actor with credible adapters across the inspected stacks |
| `ENV-03` | Finite timestamp validity | `E-TIME-01` and `E-TIME-03` in [E-time](#e-time-validity-domains) | Implementation, conflict, and recommendation | One finite interval is committed and translated into a binding without broader endpoints | Widely observed, useful, and displayable; the EIP-8141 PoC still needs an adapter fix |
| `ENV-04` | Replay-resistance outcome | `E-LIFE-02` and `E-LIFE-03` in [E-lifecycle](#e-lifecycle-commitments-replay-and-revocation) | Inference and recommendation | Root authorisation, installation, and transaction authorisation cannot be replayed outside their intended reusable or single-use domain | Every binding needs the outcome even though nonce, salt, epoch, and cache mechanisms differ |
| `ACT-01` | Exact target-selector alternatives | `E-ACT-01` in [E-action](#e-action-action-identity-and-call-shape) | Implementation, inference, and recommendation | Each alternative preserves one target-selector pair; OR applies only across enumerated alternatives | Avoids the authority-widening Cartesian product of independent targets and selectors |
| `ACT-02` | Explicit empty-calldata action | `E-ACT-02` in [E-action](#e-action-action-identity-and-call-shape) | Implementation conflict and recommendation | A target can separately allow calldata length zero; lengths one to three are rejected and named selectors do not imply empty calls | Supports plain native calls without creating a wildcard fallback; adapters need exact-length guards |
| `ACT-03` | Ordinary account `CALL` only | `E-ACT-04` in [E-action](#e-action-action-identity-and-call-shape) | Implementation and recommendation | Application execution has ordinary call semantics; delegatecall, creation, account administration, and privileged self-calls are absent | Keeps storage-context and administrative authority outside the smallest profile |
| `COMP-01` | Conjunctive restrictions on an action | `E-COMP-01` in [E-composition](#e-composition-policy-combination) | Inference and recommendation | Every applicable restriction passes; no evaluator may reinterpret an AND set as OR | Narrow common composition rule with deterministic display |
| `COMMIT-01` | Commitment to complete semantic content and versions | `E-LIFE-02` and `E-SS2-02` in [E-lifecycle](#e-lifecycle-commitments-replay-and-revocation) and [E-smart-v2](#e-smart-v2-claim-and-emissary-lifecycle) | Inference and recommendation | A deterministic commitment covers the complete granted meaning, including profile and type versions, independently of binding authorisation | Required to bind root approval to portable meaning; an existing permission ID or manager commitment is not sufficient evidence |
| `LIFE-01` | Root-controlled live revocation | `E-LIFE-01` in [E-lifecycle](#e-lifecycle-commitments-replay-and-revocation) | Implementation, inference, and recommendation | The root can make a live grant unusable; expiry and cancellation of an unused signature are different events | Essential lifecycle outcome even though each binding performs it differently |
| `CONF-01` | Strict unknown-type and version rejection | `E-WALLET-01` and `E-WALLET-02` in [E-wallet](#e-wallet-discovery-grant-return-and-display) | Security recommendation | Unknown required profiles, actor types, policy types, versions, or combinations fail at every processing stage | Prevents silent weakening by partial parsers or evaluators |
| `CONF-02` | Exact supported-configuration discovery | `E-WALLET-01` in [E-wallet](#e-wallet-discovery-grant-return-and-display) | Specification gap and recommendation | Discovery advertises exact chain-profile-actor-extension-binding tuples, not independent lists that imply a Cartesian product | A dapp must know that the whole requested configuration is enforceable before approval |
| `CONF-03` | Complete granted-object return | `E-WALLET-01` in [E-wallet](#e-wallet-discovery-grant-return-and-display) | Specification and recommendation | The response contains the exact effective authority; it does not make the dapp reconstruct changes from opaque context or rules | Required for request-to-grant comparison and safe exercise |
| `CONF-04` | Complete structured wallet display | `E-WALLET-02` in [E-wallet](#e-wallet-discovery-grant-return-and-display) | Implementation precedent and recommendation | Every effective grant field and any adjustment is deterministically renderable without interpreting an opaque module address | MetaMask proves this for a typed subset, not arbitrary policy bytes; enforceability alone is not informed authorisation |
| `CONF-05` | No implicit widening | `E-WALLET-01` in [E-wallet](#e-wallet-discovery-grant-return-and-display) | Specification conflict and recommendation | A wider or substituted result is an explicit counter-offer, never an attenuated success | ERC-7715 currently calls increases attenuation; the baseline must not |
| `EXEC-01` | Account-originated application execution | `E-BIND-01` in [E-binding](#e-binding-account-execution-and-transaction-transport) | Inference and recommendation | A conforming binding proves that authorised application calls execute from the account identity, not from an unrelated manager | Preserves application-visible account semantics across transaction systems |
| `BIND-01` | One selected live binding per grant | `E-BIND-02` in [E-binding](#e-binding-account-execution-and-transaction-transport) | Unresolved evidence and interim recommendation | Until shared state is demonstrated, one grant ID exposes one live enforcement route | Prevents duplicated stateful capacity and divergent revocation records |

## Candidate baseline projection for review

The baseline hypothesis is exactly the set of rows in the candidate mandatory table:

- envelope and lifecycle: `ENV-01` through `ENV-04` and `LIFE-01`;
- action and composition: `ACT-01` through `ACT-03` and `COMP-01`;
- commitment and conformance: `COMMIT-01` and `CONF-01` through `CONF-05`; and
- execution and binding safety: `EXEC-01` and `BIND-01`.

No asset capability is currently mandatory. Native value and direct ERC-20 caps remain important unresolved candidates. Promotion requires implementers to agree on identical charge unit, mutation point, rollback, batch, reentrancy, false-return, and display semantics. A wallet can display an asset restriction it supports without claiming that every baseline grant or binding supports it.

The projection deliberately requires a complete semantic commitment but does not select its codec, hash, domain, or ordering. It requires root-controlled live revocation but does not pretend that ERC-7715 already has complete result, status, or finality semantics. It prohibits implicit widening but forbids adjustment for any type whose equal-or-narrower relation is not yet decidable.

## Extension promotion requirements

A capability promoted to a standard extension must define:

- a stable type identifier and explicit version;
- canonical parameter encoding and semantic interpretation;
- applicability to exact action shapes;
- composition and evaluation order;
- observable state transitions, failure, reentrancy, and rollback;
- complete wallet presentation;
- a decidable equal-or-narrower relation, or an explicit prohibition on wallet adjustment;
- security considerations;
- exact chain-profile-actor-extension-binding discovery; and
- positive, negative, boundary, and adversarial conformance vectors.

Unknown extension types, versions, or required combinations must fail during discovery, request validation, grant return, import, installation, validation, and exercise.

## Decisions deliberately deferred

This union does not choose:

- a final mandatory asset subset;
- canonical policy identifiers or registry governance;
- ABI, deterministic CBOR, EIP-712, or another codec;
- a semantic hash function, domain, or collection ordering;
- one onchain evaluator interface;
- one adjustment relation across all future extensions;
- simultaneous binding state coordination;
- multichain grants;
- portable redelegation; or
- general Boolean policy composition.

Those choices follow implementer review of this classification and the standards boundary in [`STANDARDS-SHAPE.md`](./STANDARDS-SHAPE.md).
